Canada

Basic information

Quick Facts

Population: ~41 million

Capital: Ottawa

Official languages: English, French

Size: 9,984,670 km²

Currency: Canadian dollar (CAD)

Time-zone: Six main time zones (UTC−3.5 to UTC−8)

Location: Northern North America, bordered by the United States and three oceans.

Landscapes & Locations

Canada contains vast forests, mountain ranges, prairies, lakes, tundra, and rugged coastlines. It is one of the most geographically diverse countries in the world.

Notable Sightseeing Locations: Banff National Park, Niagara Falls, Jasper National Park, CN Tower

Cultural heritage

First Nations and Multiculturalism

Canada's cultural identity is shaped by Indigenous First Nations peoples, French and British colonial heritage, and waves of immigration from around the world.

Indigenous cultures, languages, and traditions continue to play a central role in the national conversation and are increasingly recognised in law and public life.

Language & Society

A Bilingual Nation

Canada has two official languages — English and French — reflecting its dual colonial heritage. Quebec is the primary French-speaking province, with Montréal as a vibrant bilingual metropolis.

Canadian society is widely regarded as one of the most open and diverse in the world, with immigration shaping cities like Vancouver, Toronto, and Calgary.
