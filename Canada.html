Basic information

Quick Facts

Canada is the second largest country in the world by total area, spanning six time zones from the Atlantic to the Pacific. Ottawa is the capital, while Toronto is the largest city.

With a population of around 40 million, Canada is known for its multicultural society, natural resources, and some of the most dramatic wilderness on the planet.

Landscapes & Locations

From the Rocky Mountain peaks of British Columbia and Alberta to the boreal forests of Ontario and the tundra of the Far North, Canada's landscapes are extraordinary in scale.

Banff, Jasper, and Algonquin national parks are among the most visited natural destinations, offering wildlife, glaciers, and sweeping mountain scenery.

Cultural heritage

First Nations and Multiculturalism

Canada's cultural identity is shaped by Indigenous First Nations peoples, French and British colonial heritage, and waves of immigration from around the world.

Indigenous cultures, languages, and traditions continue to play a central role in the national conversation and are increasingly recognised in law and public life.

Language & Society

A Bilingual Nation

Canada has two official languages — English and French — reflecting its dual colonial heritage. Quebec is the primary French-speaking province, with Montréal as a vibrant bilingual metropolis.

Canadian society is widely regarded as one of the most open and diverse in the world, with immigration shaping cities like Vancouver, Toronto, and Calgary.
