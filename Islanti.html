Basic information

Quick Facts

Population: ~399,000

Capital: Reykjavík

Official languages: Icelandic

Size: 103,000 km²

Currency: Icelandic króna (ISK)

Time-zone: GMT (UTC+0)

Location: North Atlantic Ocean, between Greenland and Europe.

Landscapes & Locations

Iceland is known for its dramatic volcanic landscape, featuring mountains, glaciers, lava fields, waterfalls, and geothermal areas. The country has very little forest cover and many rugged coastal regions.

Notable Locations: Blue Lagoon, Golden Circle, Jökulsárlón, Vatnajökull National Park

Cultural heritage

Sagas and Storytelling

Icelandic identity has deep roots in oral tradition, old Norse sagas, and a close relationship with dramatic natural landscapes.

Cultural heritage

Language and Literature

The Icelandic language has remained remarkably close to Old Norse, and literature continues to play a major role in contemporary culture.
