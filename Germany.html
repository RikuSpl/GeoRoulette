Basic information

Quick Facts

Germany is located in central Europe and is the most populous country in the EU, with around 84 million inhabitants. Berlin is the capital and largest city.

A federal republic of 16 states, Germany is a major economic and political force in Europe, known for engineering, philosophy, music, and literature.

Landscapes & Locations

Germany offers diverse scenery: the Black Forest, Bavarian Alps, Rhine Valley vineyards, Baltic and North Sea coasts, and the flat plains of the north.

Iconic landmarks include Neuschwanstein Castle, the Eagle's Nest, Cologne Cathedral, and the historic centres of cities like Heidelberg and Rothenburg ob der Tauber.

Cultural heritage

Castles, Music, and Philosophy

Germany has one of the richest cultural traditions in the world. Composers like Bach, Beethoven, and Wagner, and philosophers like Kant and Nietzsche emerged from German soil.

Folk traditions, regional festivals, and the Christmas market culture remain deeply embedded in German social life.

History

From Empire to Republic

Germany's history spans the Holy Roman Empire, Prussian unification, two World Wars, the division and reunification of East and West Germany in 1990.

Modern Germany is widely seen as a model of democratic renewal, and Berlin has become one of Europe's most culturally vibrant capitals.
