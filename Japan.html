Basic information

Quick Facts

Japan is an island nation in East Asia made up of four main islands: Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku. Tokyo is the capital and the world's most populous metropolitan area.

With a population of around 125 million, Japan blends one of the world's most advanced economies with a deeply preserved cultural heritage spanning thousands of years.

Landscapes & Locations

Japan's terrain is predominantly mountainous, with iconic peaks like Mount Fuji visible across great distances. Dense forests, hot spring villages, and coastal scenery define the rural landscape.

Cities like Kyoto, Osaka, Hiroshima, and Nara offer layered experiences of ancient temples, castle towns, and modern urban life side by side.

Cultural heritage

Between Tradition and Modernity

Japanese culture is shaped by Shinto and Buddhist traditions, expressed through shrine ceremonies, tea ceremony, ikebana flower arranging, and classical theatre forms like Noh and Kabuki.

Contemporary Japan is equally influential globally through manga, anime, architecture, cuisine, and design aesthetics.

Traditions

Seasons, Ritual, and Craft

Japanese life is closely tied to seasonal change — cherry blossom season in spring, summer matsuri festivals, autumn foliage viewing, and winter snow landscapes all carry cultural and aesthetic significance.

Craft traditions including ceramics, lacquerwork, textile dyeing, and sword-making continue to be practiced and respected as living art forms.
