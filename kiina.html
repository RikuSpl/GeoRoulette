Basic information

Quick Facts

Population: ~1.41 billion

Capital: Beijing

Official languages: Standard Chinese (Mandarin)

Size: 9,596,961 km²

Currency: Renminbi (Yuan, CNY)

Time-zone: China Standard Time (UTC+8)

Location: East Asia, bordering 14 countries including Russia, India, and Mongolia.

Landscapes & Locations

China has one of the world's most varied landscapes, including mountains, plateaus, deserts, plains, river valleys, and subtropical forests.

Notable Sightseeing Locations: Great Wall of China, Forbidden City, Terracotta Army, Li River

Cultural heritage

Five Thousand Years of Civilisation

Chinese culture encompasses philosophy, poetry, painting, calligraphy, opera, and martial arts traditions developed over millennia. Confucianism, Taoism, and Buddhism have all shaped Chinese thought and daily life.

Festivals such as Chinese New Year, the Mid-Autumn Festival, and the Dragon Boat Festival remain central to cultural life both in China and among Chinese communities worldwide.

History

Dynasties and the Modern Era

From the Qin unification in 221 BC to the Tang and Ming dynasties, China's imperial history is one of the richest in the world. The Terracotta Army, the Great Wall, and the Forbidden City are enduring symbols of this era.

The founding of the People's Republic in 1949 marked a new chapter, and China's rapid modernisation over the past four decades has transformed both the country and the global economy.
