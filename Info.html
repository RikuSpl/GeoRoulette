About us

We are a small team of three passionate creators who came together with a shared interest in travel, technology, and discovering the world. Our goal with GeoRoulette is to make exploring different countries and cultures fun, simple, and interactive. GeoRoulette is a platform designed to inspire curiosity and encourage people to discover new places, cultures, and interesting facts.
As a small and growing team, we each bring our own ideas and skills to the project. From design and development to content and planning, we work together to turn our ideas into something people can enjoy. GeoRoulette is our first step toward creating a more engaging way to explore the world, one country at a time.
