Basic information

Quick Facts

Population: ~38 million

Capital: Rabat

Official languages: Arabic, Amazigh (Berber)

Size: 446,550 km²

Currency: Moroccan dirham (MAD)

Time-zone: GMT+1 (UTC+1)

Location: North Africa, bordering Algeria and the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea.

Landscapes & Locations

Morocco features the Atlas Mountains, coastal plains, fertile valleys, and parts of the Sahara Desert. The landscape varies greatly between regions.

Notable Sightseeing Locations: Marrakesh Medina, Sahara Desert, Chefchaouen, Aït Benhaddou

Cultural heritage

Craft Traditions and Medinas

Moroccan identity is shaped by Amazigh, Arab, African, and Mediterranean influences. Historic medinas, intricate zellige tilework, and artisan craftsmanship are central to daily life and design.

Language and identity

Language and Literature

Arabic and Amazigh are official languages, with French also widely used in education, business, and media. Poetry, oral storytelling, and contemporary writing remain important forms of cultural expression.
